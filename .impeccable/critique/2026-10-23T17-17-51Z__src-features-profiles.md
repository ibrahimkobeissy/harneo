## Design Specificity
Authored for Harneo, and the prototype fidelity is high. The strong moves are product-specific: the live "project it produces" tree with per-file provenance ("From X . source", "Generated by Harneo"), the radio list of whole Settings templates with a `deny/ask/allow` summary and an eye to view the JSON, the `${VAR}`-only env rows that refuse a real secret and say so, "pulled in by" rows, and the half-wired bundle confirm. Another tool could not reuse these unchanged. The weaknesses are accumulation and load. The cart is a 420px column that has to carry five panels, and the picker rows, which are the shop shelf, carry the least information (trust and source are truncated and unflagged) even though trust is the product's thesis. The Profiles list is the plainest screen: four near-identical cards, no recency, no delete, no sort.

Detector (CLI on source): 1 finding, `design-system-radius` 9px toggle radius (Builder.module.css:507), advisory. Detector (CDP): `tiny-text` 11.5px on every picker `.srcline` (all light and dark states), `low-contrast` 4.4:1 (#64748b on #eff6ff) on the picker rows that are already added (the srcline sits on the blue-soft row wash), `straggler` advisory on the library detail's first paragraph, `overused-font` Inter on every page (false positive: DESIGN.md specifies Inter). Profiles list, clone and import dialogs, bundle confirm and leave guard: clean. Measured (computed): picker srcline 4.37:1 light on added rows (carries the trust number); `.why`, panel notes, Settings summary 4.76:1 (pass, no headroom); Unsaved badge 4.51:1 light (pass, no headroom); notice ok 4.57:1; toggle off track `--line2` #cbd5e1 on white about 1.5:1 and #2c3a55 on #131c31 about 1.4:1 (fails 3:1 non-text); picker Add/Added buttons 26px high, cart remove X 33x26. Dark mode otherwise passes.

## Priority Issues
1. [P1] The cart is one long scroll and Save, the unsaved state and the tree scroll away. Cart.tsx:81-107 puts name, version and Save at the top of a `.cart` that scrolls inside itself (Builder.module.css:228-238, max-height calc(100vh - 140px)). With 8 picked artifacts "In this profile" (Cart.tsx:109-150) is 300px tall and Settings starts at y=566 at 1440; the tree starts below 1000px. After any scroll the user cannot see Save or "v2 -> v3 on save". Totals (Cart.tsx:163-184) repeats the list and the tree's "12 files".
2. [P1] Importing a profile file shows no provenance or trust for the new artifacts. ImportDialog.tsx:84-94 lists name and kind only, under "New: added to your library", for artifacts that come from a stranger's file. Harneo's whole thesis is proof of what is inside; this is the one place an unknown artifact enters with no audit signal, source or file count, and "Import profile" is the primary button.
3. [P1] Trust is hidden or truncated where the user decides. Picker.tsx:164-170 builds one line "bundle of N . source . trust T" that `.srcline` truncates with an ellipsis (Builder.module.css:220-226), so at 1440 the bundle rows lose the trust number; a trust-41 skill shows as plain grey text; the only warning is a toast that auto-dismisses after 6 s (ProfileBuilder.tsx:204-209, 237-241). Once added, the cart list and the tree show nothing about it. The srcline is also 11.5px and 4.37:1 on added rows.
4. [P2] Path conflicts are visible but not actionable. ProjectTree.tsx:48-55 prints "1 path is claimed twice: <path>" without the two artifact names (they only appear after clicking the file, FileInfo line 156), Save stays enabled, and nothing says what to do (remove one). The conflict is the one hard error this screen can show.
5. [P2] A new profile's first save is silent and the destination is a remount. ProfileBuilder.tsx:294-299 navigates to `/profiles/<id>` with `replace`, `Loader` is keyed by id, so the "Saved" notice state is lost; the user sees the version text change from "new . v1" to "v1 . saved" and the title change, nothing else.
6. [P2] Confirm dialogs have the wrong weight and wording. LeaveGuard.tsx:102-111: title "Leave without saving?", sub says the changes stay as a draft (so nothing is lost), body "Stay to save them first.", buttons "Cancel" and a blue primary "Leave": the stay button is called Cancel and the sentence contradicts the title. RemoveDialog.tsx:27-33 submit text "Remove code-reviewer and code-review-and-quality" is 45 characters on a primary blue button where the prototype uses danger; Dialog.tsx:160-165 has no variant prop.
7. [P2] Picker has no sense of size. Picker.tsx:34 `LIMIT = 40`, no total, no "showing 40 of 212", no way to page; sorted by sessions so a new artifact is out of reach except by search. Added rows and not-added rows interleave with no "Added only" filter, and the segment bar wraps CLAUDE.md onto a second row inside its bordered box at 960.
8. [P2] Manual-only is a setting the library never shows. ItemCard.tsx, ItemTable.tsx and ItemMeta.tsx have no marker, so the Invocation filter returns cards that do not say why they matched, and the cart's "manual only" chip is the only place it appears. The toggle in the detail panel (ProfileSections.module.css `.switch`, Builder.module.css:501-509 `.toggle`) uses `--line2` as its off track (1.5:1).
9. [P2] The Add to... menu mixes collections and profiles in one flat list ("Profile: Light", "Profile: Full stack", AddToMenu.tsx:196-210) with no heading and no cap; at 10 profiles it is a long unlabeled list, and the in-menu status line is the only confirmation.
10. [P2] Profiles list gaps. ProfilesScreen.tsx:122-150: no loading state (blank), the error state has no Retry, the empty state has no button (prototype has "New profile" there), no delete or rename anywhere, the export result is a full-width banner that pushes the grid down by 60px and stays until dismissed.

## What's Working
- The live tree. Files appear as you add, each with provenance and a conflict mark; "The project it produces" is exactly the shopping-cart UX CONTEXT.md section 6 asked for.
- The secret guard and the bundle confirm. Typing a real token into an env row is refused, the old placeholder comes back and the message explains why; removing a bundle member explains the half-wired result. These are the product's character.
- Clone and Import dialogs: one name field, a sub line saying what will happen ("saved as its own profile at v1", "3 of 5 artifacts are already in your library"), a name-clash warning with a pre-filled "(imported)" name. Dark and light are both clean.

## Persona Red Flags
**Alex (power user, 30 artifacts)**: Add is a mouse-only button per row; no select-and-add-all for a search result; no keyboard route from the picker to the cart; Save scrolls out of view; picker capped at 40 rows; export result banner must be dismissed. Cannot delete a profile.
**Jordan (first-timer who has never heard of a bundle)**: "Settings template" and "exactly one" are explained, but "bundle of 2" is not (the lead says "adds its whole bundle"); "pulled in by" is clear; the tree shows files with no explanation of `.mcp.json` or `HARNESS.md`; "Totals" counts "Settings template 1 / MCP servers 0" next to "Files written 12" with no relation. After the first save nothing says it worked.
**Sam (keyboard and screen reader)**: "Added" buttons carry aria-label "Remove X" (Picker.tsx:181-183) so the visible name "Added" is not in the accessible name (2.5.3); no `aria-pressed` on the add state; toggle off track 1.5:1 and no On/Off text; picker srcline 4.4:1 on added rows; the 6 s trust toast is `role=status` and disappears; cart remove buttons are 33x26. The leave dialog's Esc and Tab behave.

## Minor Observations
- `.cart` max-height `calc(100vh - 140px)` leaves a dead 48px strip under the column and clips the last panel mid-row with a hard edge and no fade.
- The notice is `position: fixed` and centered on the viewport, so at 960 it covers the last picker row's trust text.
- Version text "v2 . saved" and the Unsaved badge both state dirtiness; the page title repeats the name field value ("Heavy" and "Profile name: Heavy").
- Settings and MCP panels say "No Settings templates in the library yet" and "No servers." without a pointer to Import or Library.
- ProfileCard: the Rust service card (no description) leaves a 30px hole; "Used in 4 projects" is not a link to those projects.
- `border-radius: 9px` on `.toggle` (Builder.module.css:507) is off the DESIGN.md scale; use the pill token.
- Create profile from a collection creates and navigates in one step, but the collection's settings and MCP items are silently skipped (spec) with no line in the new profile saying so.
- Profile load error: "Profile not found" with the raw error string.

## Questions to Consider

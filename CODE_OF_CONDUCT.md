# Code of Conduct

Harneo adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want taking part in Harneo to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

- **Do:** be respectful and patient, give and accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the project and its users, own your mistakes.
- **Don't:** use sexualized language or imagery, troll, insult or make personal or political attacks, harass anyone publicly or privately, or publish others' private information without their explicit permission.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@ibrahimkobeissy](https://github.com/ibrahimkobeissy), privately, or use GitHub's *Report content* option on the comment or issue. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).

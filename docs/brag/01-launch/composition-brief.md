## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished
- Creative direction: Linear / Vercel-grade product film; the real UI does the talking.
- Interpretation: few words, long holds, transitions caused by something on screen, one accent colour.
- Angle: Harneo is the quiet tool that refuses to lose your files; the red refit warning is the climax.
- Hook: a typed `cp -r ../old-repo/.claude .` that duplicates down the frame; "Copied by hand from repo to repo."
- Outro / punchline: logo, "A harness manager for Claude Code.", MIT, GitHub URL.
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler, any redesign of the app UI, invented numbers.

## Storyboard
Use the storyboard in `docs/brag/brag-plan.md` as the creative contract.

1. Hook — 3.0 s — typed cp line, faded copies, "Copied by hand, repo to repo."
2. Reveal — 3.0 s — logo + "Your Claude Code harness, fitted to every project."
3. Import audit — 5.0 s — Library window, "Every artifact audited on import.", audit card with two Critical findings, "1 artifact held back for critical findings."
4. Profile — 3.0 s — profile editor, simulated Add click, "Compose a profile once."
5. Refit — 6.0 s — push into red warning, Keep click, calm after-state, "Previewed. Backed up. Reversible."
6. Outro — 4.0 s — logo, tagline, MIT, URL.

## Audio
- Audio role: warm bed with sparse accents.
- Audio arc: sparse hook → kick on reveal → pad-only dip under the warning → resolve on the logo → fade.
- Music: `assets/music/harneo-theme.wav`, an original composition generated by `docs/brag/music/compose.py` (no attribution needed).
- Music treatment: full volume bed, fade-out 23.0-24.0 s.
- Music cue guidance: beats every 0.50 s from 0; bar 2.00 s; strong cues 3.00, 18.00, 20.00 s (`assets/music/harneo-theme.cues.json`).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; per-frame RMS (`assets/music/harneo-theme.rms.js`) drives the blue glow behind the logo in the reveal and the outro.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed hook (key ticks), audit card arrival (card slide), Add and Keep clicks, logo landing (soft impact).
- SFX: Kenney CC0 files from /brag's library, low-HF-risk picks, mixed under the music.
- Audio files: copied into `composition/assets/`.
